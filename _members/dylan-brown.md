Dylan is from Albuquerque, New Mexico and completed his bachelor’s degree in chemical engineering at the University of New Mexico. He earned his PhD in chemical engineering from Northwestern University working under Julius Lucks. At Northwestern, he created new cell-free biosensors for human and environmental health monitoring. In the Demirer lab, Dylan will develop protein delivery technology to expand targets for plant genetic engineering. Outside of the lab he enjoys making art, cooking, concerts, and outdoor activities.
