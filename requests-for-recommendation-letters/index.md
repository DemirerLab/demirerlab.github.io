# Requests for Recommendation Letters

Recommendation letter requests are welcome from students and researchers who have taken courses from Prof. Demirer or who have performed research in the Demirer lab. I also welcome requests from graduate students who have served as teaching assistants for my courses, or students with whom I’ve worked for outreach activities. Please request your letter no later than two weeks prior to your deadline with the following information:

1. An unofficial transcript (if undergraduate student)

2. Your CV or resume including awards, research presentations, and publications if applicable

3. Clear instructions on how to submit your recommendation letter & its deadline

4. A bullet-point list of items you would like to have included in your recommendation letter

This information will help draft a strong letter of recommendation that highlights your individual strengths.
