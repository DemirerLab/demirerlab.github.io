## Graduate Students

Prospective graduate students should apply to the relevant Caltech Ph.D. programs.

Incoming Caltech graduate students interested in rotations are encouraged to **[contact Gözde](mailto:gdemirer@caltech.edu)**.

## Postdoctoral Fellows

Positions available for postdoctoral fellows with experience and interest in **nanotechnology**, **plant biotechnology and genetic engineering**, and/or **rhizosphere engineering.** Please **[contact Gözde](mailto:gdemirer@caltech.edu)**[ with a CV and cover letter](mailto:gdemirer@caltech.edu).

## Undergraduate Students

Demirer Lab is interested in research mentoring of Caltech undergrads from any division during academic terms and/or summer. If interested, **[contact Gözde](mailto:gdemirer@caltech.edu)**.
